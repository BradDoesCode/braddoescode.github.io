How I got here

I didn't start out in software. After ten years in retail and team-leading roles, I got into code in 2022 by running a British-themed FiveM roleplay server for GTA V. I wrote its scripts in Lua and ran the community, which grew to more than 1,000 members. At its peak, the server was full at 64 players every night. It's what made me want to code for a living, and it gave me a head start when I did.
